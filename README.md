# I Build.

My name is Landon Boggs. I'm an entrepreneur. I founded and run
**BuyIt Ventures**, an equity development firm that builds and backs new
companies alongside the experts who run them.

I think from first principles, and I work as the Integrator for the Visionary:
I'm the one who turns ideas into action. My approach is systems-first. I
understand the foundation, own it, then build on top, so each venture can
strengthen the next. I want the work to be good *and* the economics to make
sense, and I don't see those as in tension. A venture has to be worth building
and worth the return it aims for.

Based in Virginia. 🌐 [landonboggs.com](https://landonboggs.com)

## The firm

**[BuyIt Ventures](https://buyitventures.com)**, an equity development firm
with its own fund.

The fund creates and funds new companies by partnering with technical experts
in their field, from home services and food service to software and real
estate. Operators who know and care stay at the helm, and the firm's resources
handle everything else. Each company is scaled toward one of two outcomes: a
future exit, or a long-haul investment in the core idea.

## Core values

People, and the different ways our minds work, are what matter most. Everything
else exists to serve them: the right food, affordable places to live and work,
and platforms to build on.

- **People.** Individuals come first. My job is to build the ecosystem that
  lets people think big and actually achieve it.
- **Creativity.** It has to be allowed to reign. The best work comes from people
  given room to make something that didn't exist before.
- **Knowledge.** Learning, and passing it on, is essential. It compounds when
  it's shared.
